# Pun jokes

Style notes:
- Pure wordplay — a double meaning or homophone is the entire joke.
- No setup/punchline structure required; can be a single sentence.
- Cleverness matters more than being laugh-out-loud funny.

Examples:

- I'm on a seafood diet. I see food and I eat it.
- Time flies like an arrow. Fruit flies like a banana.
- I used to be a baker, but I couldn't make enough dough.
- A bicycle can't stand on its own because it's two-tired.
- The math teacher called in sick with algebra.
